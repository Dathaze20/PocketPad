# PocketPad Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 2026*

PocketPad turns your phone into a Bluetooth game controller. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** PocketPad does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no network code other than the local Bluetooth and USB connections that carry your button presses.

## Permissions we use and why

- **Bluetooth (Connect / Advertise):** used solely to pair with and send controller input to the device you choose (TV, tablet, PC). No Bluetooth information leaves your phone.
- **USB host access:** used solely to read input from a game controller you physically plug into your phone.
- **Vibration:** used solely for haptic button feedback.

## Data stored on your device

PocketPad remembers only your preferences (chosen controller style, vibration level, and the Bluetooth address of the last device you connected to) in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Children

PocketPad collects no data from anyone, including children.
